# EcoLens AI – Intelligent E-Waste Quantification & Circular Economy Platform

## Executive Summary

EcoLens AI is an AI-powered digital platform that enables governments, educational institutions, industries, and recycling organizations to intelligently quantify, predict, monitor, and manage electronic waste. The platform combines Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Geospatial Analytics, and Sustainability Intelligence to transform raw e-waste data into actionable insights.

Unlike traditional inventory management systems, EcoLens AI provides predictive analytics, hotspot visualization, intelligent recommendations, sustainability scoring, and decision-support tools through a single integrated platform.

# Problem Statement

India generates millions of tonnes of electronic waste every year. Most organizations still rely on manual inventories, spreadsheets, or disconnected systems to track electronic assets and waste generation.

Current challenges include:

- Lack of accurate e-waste inventory
- No predictive analysis of future waste generation
- Inefficient recycling planning
- Poor visibility of collection hotspots
- Limited sustainability measurement
- Absence of intelligent decision support

These limitations result in inefficient resource utilization, environmental pollution, and missed recycling opportunities.

---

# Proposed Solution

EcoLens AI provides a unified AI-powered platform that enables organizations to:

- Quantify electronic waste
- Predict future e-waste generation
- Analyse electronic devices using Computer Vision
- Recommend optimal recycling strategies
- Visualize e-waste hotspots through GIS
- Measure sustainability impact
- Simulate future recycling scenarios

The platform enables stakeholders to move from reactive waste management to proactive, data-driven planning.
